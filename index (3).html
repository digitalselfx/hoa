FAQ

Common questions

Are you a contractor?

No. We don't perform construction work. We define scope, qualify vendors, compare proposals and oversee the contractor on the association's behalf.

Will you just find us the cheapest contractor?

No — the lowest bid isn't always the best value. We help boards make large vendor decisions with better information: what's included, what's excluded, warranty terms and where change orders are likely.

We already have a management company. Why would we need you?

Most CAMs manage day-to-day operations across many communities. We focus solely on your capital project, working alongside your manager — not replacing them.

Can you help if the contractor is already hired?

Yes. Independent Oversight is designed for exactly that — inspections, progress reports, change-order review and final verification without rebidding the project.

Do contractors pay you anything?

Never. Our only client is the HOA, and we disclose our fee structure in writing for every engagement.

What does the free consultation cover?

A 30-minute conversation about your planned project: budget, how many proposals you have, who wrote the scope, who will oversee the work and who will review change orders. You'll leave with a clear picture of your project risks — whether or not you hire us.
